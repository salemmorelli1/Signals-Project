Simulation conclusion

The corrected blind joint torus-valued SSM completed the full paired factorial simulation. The following results come from the committed row-level evidence, with ESS measured before resampling.

Accuracy

No clear primary architecture effect

Mean frequency MSE was 219.21 Hz² for the surrogate and 218.43 Hz² for the analytical score. Across 100 seed profiles, the raw paired difference was 0.78 Hz², with an unadjusted 95% t CI [-14.23, 15.79]. The primary log-MSE architecture test gives F(1, 99) = 0.30, pHolm = 1.

Physical conditions

Prespecified factorial tests

Primary Holm-adjusted p values: SNR 1.162e-09; channel 1.892e-07; SNR × channel 0.006392. These tests refer to controlled simulation conditions.

Claim boundary

Real-data inference remains

Recorded timing was 2.840 versus 2.692 ms/sample under fixed analytical-first execution. That timing contrast is descriptive, not causal. WiSig preparation is complete; model performance on WiSig remains untested.
